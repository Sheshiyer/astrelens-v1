- Label: "Deeper Vedic precision"
- Description: "27 refined divisions"

**Ring 5: PADAS — 108 Micro-Segments**
- Visual: Ultra-fine geometry

## Animation Notes (For Video Version)

**Sequence:**
1. Title fades in (0.5s)
2. Problem panel slides up (0.5s)
3. Grand Clock rings bloom outward sequentially (2s total):
   - Ring 1 (Self): 0.3s
   - Ring 2 (Houses): 0.3s
   - Ring 3 (Signs): 0.3s
   - Ring 4 (Nakshatras): 0.3s
   - Ring 5 (Padas): 0.3s
   - Pause: 0.5s
4. Planets drop into position (0.8s, spring physics)
5. Aspect beams draw on (2s, ease-in-out)
6. Scale modes highlight one by one (2s)
7. CTA fades in with glow (0.5s)

**Continuous:**
- Subtle particle drift (stars)
- Gentle breathing animation on center glow
- Slow rotation of outer rings (0.5 RPM)
