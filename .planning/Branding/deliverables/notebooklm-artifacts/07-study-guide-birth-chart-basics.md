# AstroLens Study Guide: Birth Chart Basics

**Level:** Beginner  
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes  
**Goal:** Understand your chart using the Grand Clock visualization

---

## Module 1: Introduction to Birth Charts (5 minutes)

### What is a Birth Chart?

A birth chart (or natal chart) is a map of where all the planets were in the sky at the exact moment you were born. Think of it as a cosmic selfie taken at your birth.

**Key Concept:** Your chart doesn't change. It's a fixed blueprint. What changes is how the current sky (transits) interacts with your chart over time.

### Why Visualize It?

Traditional charts are 2D tables with symbols. The Grand Clock makes it 3D, spatial, and intuitive. You can:
- See relationships between planets instantly
- Understand house positions at a glance
- Feel the chart as a living system

**Exercise:** Open AstroLens and look at your Grand Clock. Don't worry about understanding everything yet. Just observe the shapes, colors, and patterns.

## Module 2: The Center — You (5 minutes)

### The Ascendant (Rising Sign)

**Location:** Center of the Grand Clock (Ring 1)

Your Ascendant is the zodiac sign that was rising on the eastern horizon when you were born. It represents:
- How you appear to others (first impressions)
- Your physical body and vitality
- The "lens" through which you experience life

**Visual Cue:** In AstroLens, the center glows with your rising sign's elemental color.

### Finding Your Ascendant

**In AstroLens:**
1. Look at the center of your Grand Clock
2. See which sign's color dominates the center
3. Tap the center for details

**Exercise:** Write down your Ascendant sign. Read the description in the app. Does it resonate with how others perceive you?

## Module 3: The Houses — Where in Life (10 minutes)

### What Are Houses?

**Location:** Ring 2 of the Grand Clock

The 12 houses represent different areas of your life. They're like rooms in your cosmic home.

### The 12 Houses

| House | Name | Life Area | Angular |
|-------|------|-----------|---------|
| 1st | House of Self | Identity, body, appearance | ASC |
| 2nd | House of Resources | Money, values, possessions | |
| 3rd | House of Communication | Siblings, short trips, learning | |
| 4th | House of Home | Family, roots, foundation | IC |
| 5th | House of Creativity | Romance, children, art | |
| 6th | House of Service | Health, routine, work | |
| 7th | House of Partnership | Marriage, contracts, others | DSC |
| 8th | House of Transformation | Shared resources, death, intimacy | |
| 9th | House of Expansion | Travel, philosophy, higher ed | |
| 10th | House of Career | Public status, achievement | MC |
| 11th | House of Community | Friends, groups, hopes | |
| 12th | House of Unconscious | Secrets, solitude, karma | |

**Angular Houses** (1, 4, 7, 10) are the "power points" of your chart. Planets here have extra prominence.

### Visual Pattern

In the Grand Clock:
- **House 1** starts where your Ascendant is
- Houses count **clockwise** (opposite of zodiac signs!)
- **House 4** (bottom) = home and roots
- **House 7** (left) = partnerships
- **House 10** (top) = career and public status

**Exercise:** Find which house your Sun is in. What life area does it illuminate?

## Module 4: The Signs — How It Behaves (10 minutes)

### What Are Signs?

**Location:** Ring 3 of the Grand Clock

The 12 zodiac signs are like different "flavors" or energies. They color how planets express themselves.

### The Four Elements

**Fire Signs** (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)
- Energy: Active, enthusiastic, inspired
- Traits: Passionate, courageous, spontaneous
- In Grand Clock: Warm oranges and reds

**Earth Signs** (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)
- Energy: Practical, grounded, material
- Traits: Reliable, sensual, hardworking
- In Grand Clock: Greens and browns

**Air Signs** (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)
- Energy: Intellectual, social, communicative
- Traits: Curious, diplomatic, innovative
- In Grand Clock: Yellows and blues

**Water Signs** (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)
- Energy: Emotional, intuitive, flowing
- Traits: Nurturing, intense, compassionate
- In Grand Clock: Blues and purples

### Modalities (Qualities)

**Cardinal** (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn)
- Initiators, leaders, starters
- Angular houses (1, 4, 7, 10)

**Fixed** (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius)
- Sustainers, stabilizers, finishers
- Succedent houses (2, 5, 8, 11)

**Mutable** (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces)
- Adaptable, flexible, transitional
- Cadent houses (3, 6, 9, 12)

**Exercise:** What's your Sun sign? Its element and modality? How does that show up in your life?

## Module 5: The Planets — The Actors (10 minutes)

### Personal Planets (Fast-Moving)

These planets change signs quickly and represent personal expression:

**Sun ☉**
- Represents: Core identity, ego, life force
- Orbit: 1 year
- Key question: "Who am I?"
- In Grand Clock: Golden orb, prominent

**Moon ☽**
- Represents: Emotions, instincts, needs
- Orbit: 28 days
- Key question: "What do I need?"
- In Grand Clock: Silver-white orb

**Mercury ☿**
- Represents: Communication, thinking, learning
- Orbit: 88 days
- Key question: "How do I think and communicate?"
- In Grand Clock: Green orb

**Venus ♀**
- Represents: Love, beauty, values, attraction
- Orbit: 225 days
- Key question: "What do I value and love?"
- In Grand Clock: Pink orb

**Mars ♂**
- Represents: Action, drive, assertion, conflict
- Orbit: 2 years
- Key question: "How do I take action?"
- In Grand Clock: Red orb

### Social Planets (Bridge personal and collective)

**Jupiter ♃**
- Represents: Expansion, growth, wisdom, luck
- Orbit: 12 years
- Key question: "Where do I grow and expand?"
- In Grand Clock: Yellow orb

**Saturn ♄**
- Represents: Structure, discipline, limitation, maturity
- Orbit: 29 years
- Key question: "What must I master through effort?"
- In Grand Clock: Grey orb

### Transpersonal Planets (Collective/Generational)

**Uranus ♅, Neptune ♆, Pluto ♇**
- Represent generational themes
- Slower orbits (7-248 years)
- Personal by house placement

**Exercise:** Find your Sun, Moon, and Rising in AstroLens. These three form your "Big Three" — the core of your personality.

## Module 6: Aspects — The Relationships (10 minutes)

### What Are Aspects?

Aspects are angular relationships between planets. They show how different energies in your chart interact.

### Major Aspects

**Conjunction (0°)** — Fusion
- Planets within ~10° of each other
- Energies blend and intensify
- Color in AstroLens: Gold
- Example: Sun conjunct Mercury = mind and identity merged

**Sextile (60°)** — Opportunity
- Flowing, cooperative energy
- Requires some effort to activate
- Color in AstroLens: Green
- Example: Venus sextile Mars = balance between love and desire

**Square (90°)** — Challenge
- Tension, friction, growth
- Dynamic but uncomfortable
- Color in AstroLens: Red
- Example: Sun square Saturn = identity vs. limitation

**Trine (120°)** — Flow
- Natural harmony and talent
- Easy energy, sometimes lazy
- Color in AstroLens: Blue
- Example: Moon trine Jupiter = emotional abundance

**Opposition (180°)** — Balance
- Polarity, tension, partnership
- Requires integration of opposites
- Color in AstroLens: Purple
- Example: Mars opposition Saturn = action vs. restraint

### Reading Aspects

**In AstroLens:**
1. Look for colored beams connecting planets
2. Tap a beam to see the aspect details
3. Read the AI explanation

**Pattern Recognition:**
- Many blue trines = natural talents, but may lack drive
- Many red squares = challenges, but great growth potential
- Cluster of conjunctions = focused energy in one area

**Exercise:** Find one trine and one square in your chart. What do they tell you about your natural gifts and growth areas?

## Module 7: Putting It All Together (10 minutes)

### The Formula

**Planet + Sign + House = Expression**

Example: "Saturn in Capricorn in the 10th house"
- Planet (Saturn): Discipline, structure, delay
- Sign (Capricorn): Ambition, responsibility, mastery
- House (10th): Career, public status, achievement

= "Career success through sustained effort and mastery of public responsibility"

### Practice Reading

**Try these in AstroLens:**

1. Find your **Moon**
   - What sign? (How you emote)
   - What house? (Where you need nurturing)

2. Find **Saturn**
   - What sign? (How you experience limitation)
   - What house? (Where you must mature)

3. Find your **dominant planet**
   - Which planet has the most aspects?
   - Which house has the most planets?

### Your Chart Story

Every chart tells a story. Look for:
- **Concentration:** Many planets in one house = major life theme
- **Emphasis:** Planets in angular houses (1, 4, 7, 10) = prominent expression
- **Patterns:** Grand trine, T-square, stellium = special configurations

**Exercise:** Ask the AI tutor: "Walk me through my chart story." Listen to how the elements connect.

## Module 8: Time and Transits (Optional — Advanced)

### The Natal Layer vs. Transit Layer

**Natal (Birth Chart):** Fixed. Who you are. Solid colors in AstroLens.

**Transits (Current Sky):** Moving. What's happening now. Translucent colors.

### Key Transits to Watch

**Saturn Return** (~age 29, 58)
- Saturn returns to birth position
- Major life restructuring
- "Adulting" checkpoint

**Jupiter Return** (~every 12 years)
- Jupiter returns to birth position
- Expansion and new beginnings
- Growth opportunities

### Using the Time Scrubber

**In AstroLens:**
1. Find the timeline at the bottom
2. Drag from birth to today
3. Watch transit planets move
4. Stop at major dates in your life
5. See what transits were active

**Exercise:** Scrub to a major life event. What transits were happening? Do you see the correlation?
