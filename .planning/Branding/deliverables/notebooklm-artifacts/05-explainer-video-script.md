# AstroLens Explainer Video Script

**Duration:** 90 seconds  
**Format:** 30-second hook + 60-second explanation  
**Tone:** Grounded yet wondrous, curious and inviting  
**Visual Style:** Cinematic AR visualization, smooth camera movements

---

## Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)

### Visual
[Dark screen. Single point of light appears at center. Expands into a complex 2D astrological chart—circles, glyphs, numbers. Chart rotates. User's confused face reflected in the glass.]

### Voiceover
"For thousands of years, astrologers have used charts like this to understand our connection to the cosmos."

[2D chart dissolves into a flat, lifeless diagram]

"But there's a problem. These charts are flat."

[A hand swipes the 2D chart away. Reveals glowing 3D Grand Clock floating in space]

"Your cosmic blueprint isn't flat. Neither should your app be."

### On-screen text
**AstroLens — See Astrology in AR**

---

## Scene 2: The Grand Clock Revealed (0:30 - 0:50)

### Visual
[Camera orbits around the Grand Clock. Slow push-in. We see the 5 concentric rings materialize one by one]

**Ring 1:** Center glows — "You"
**Ring 2:** 12 house wedges bloom outward
**Ring 3:** 12 zodiac sign arcs color the space
**Ring 4:** 27 nakshatra lines appear as silver threads
**Ring 5:** 108 padas create fine geometry

Planets drop into position as luminous orbs. Aspect beams connect them like constellations.

### Voiceover
"Meet the Grand Clock. Five rings. From your core identity at the center, through the twelve houses of your life, to the subtlest cosmic divisions."

[Camera zooms into a planet. Shows Saturn in the 10th house]

"Every planet. Every house. Every connection. Visible at a glance."

---

## Scene 3: AR Scale Modes (0:50 - 1:10)

### Visual
[Rapid montage of scale modes]

**Palm Mode:** Hand holds miniature Grand Clock
**Desk Mode:** Chart spreads on table, user leans in
**Room Mode:** User walks between towering ring structures
**Dome Mode:** Full cosmic immersion, ceiling becomes stars

### Voiceover
"Experience your chart at any scale. Hold it in your hand. Explore it on your desk. Walk through it at life size. Or immerse yourself completely."

[Scene settles on Desk Mode. User taps a planet]

"Same chart. Same logic. Just… more visible."

---

## Scene 4: AI Visual Tutor (1:10 - 1:30)

### Visual
[User asks a question. AI response appears as subtle UI]

User taps Saturn. Saturn glows. House 10 highlights. Aspect beams appear. Dasha hand rotates.

AI voice (warm, knowledgeable): "See this grey orb? That's your Saturn in the 10th house of career. Notice the discipline it brings to your public life."

[Timeline scrubber appears. User drags. Transit planets animate around the static natal chart]

### Voiceover
"And when you have questions, our AI Visual Tutor doesn't just tell you—it shows you. Highlighting exactly what to look at, teaching you to read your own chart."

[Timelapse animation: planets orbit, dasha hand ticks, years fly by]

"Watch your life in motion. From birth to now. See how the cosmos dances around your fixed stars."

---

## Scene 5: The Closing (1:30 - 1:90)

### Visual
[Camera pulls back through all scale modes—Room → Desk → Palm → Logo]

Grand Clock glows beautifully. User smiles, understanding dawning on their face.

Multiple users sharing their charts, pointing, discussing.

App Store badge appears.

### Voiceover
"AstroLens. The first spatial computing tool for cosmic self-discovery."

"Your birth chart, finally visible."

"See Astrology in AR."

### On-screen text
**Coming Q3 2024**
**astrolens.app**
**Pre-order on the App Store**

## Production Notes

### Music
- 0:00 - 0:30: Mysterious, building anticipation
- 0:30 - 1:10: Sweeping, orchestral, wonder-filled
- 1:10 - 1:30: Warm, conversational, educational
- 1:30 - 1:90: Uplifting, hopeful, inspiring

**Reference tracks:** 
- Interstellar soundtrack (Hans Zimmer)
- Apple product launch music
- Planetarium ambient tones

### Sound Design
- **0:08:** Whoosh as 2D chart dissolves
- **0:15:** Ethereal chime as Grand Clock appears
- **0:35:** Gentle "bloom" sound as each ring materializes
- **0:45:** Satisfying "drop" as planets fall into place
- **1:05:** Soft whoosh between scale modes
- **1:20:** UI interaction sounds (subtle, premium)
- **1:35:** Build to final crescendo

### Color Grading
- Deep cosmic blues and purples
- Warm gold accents (planets, highlights)
- Rich contrast, cinematic blacks
- Lens flare on light sources

### Typography
- On-screen text: SF Pro Display Bold
- Subtitles (if any): SF Pro Text
- All text minimal, brief, impactful

### Device Requirements
- iPhone 15 Pro (for AR demo)
- Clean hands, good lighting
- Various surfaces (table, floor, open space)
