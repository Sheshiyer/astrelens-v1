- **Psychographics:** Wants understanding, not fortune-telling
- **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by traditional apps
- **Goals:** Visual intuition, self-discovery

### Co-Star: The Social Astrologer
- **Demographics:** 18-30, social media active
- **Psychographics:** Wants to share, compare, discuss

- **Demographics:** 25-45, introspective
- **Psychographics:** Wants psychological insight
- **Pain Points:** Finds most apps superficial
- **Goals:** Deep self-understanding

### Sanctuary: The Serious Student

### TimePassages: The Professional

## Conclusion

AstroLens occupies a unique competitive position as the only app combining:
- True 3D/AR visualization
- AI-guided visual learning
- Professional astrology depth
- Premium spatial computing experience

While competitors excel in social features (Co-Star), psychological depth (The Pattern), or professional tools (TimePassages), none address the core gap in visual intuition building.

**Strategic recommendation:** AstroLens should maintain AR/AI leadership while selectively adding social features to compete for Co-Star's audience.
