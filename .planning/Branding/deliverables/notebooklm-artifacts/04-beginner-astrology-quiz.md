Test your knowledge of birth chart basics using the AstroLens Grand Clock visualization system.

## Part 1: The Grand Clock Structure

### Question 1
**What is at the center of the Grand Clock visualization?**

A) The Sun
B) The Moon
C) The Ascendant (Self)
D) The North Node

**Correct Answer: C)** The Ascendant (Self) — Your rising sign represents your core identity and serves as the anchor point for the entire chart.

---

### Question 2
**How many houses are in a birth chart?**

A) 10
B) 12
C) 7
D) 9

**Correct Answer: B)** 12 — The birth chart is divided into 12 houses, each representing different life areas.

---

### Question 3
**In the Grand Clock, which ring shows the 12 zodiac signs?**

A) Ring 1 (center)
B) Ring 2
C) Ring 3
D) Ring 5

**Correct Answer: C)** Ring 3 — The rings are: 1-Self, 2-Houses, 3-Signs, 4-Nakshatras, 5-Padas.

---

### Question 4
**What is the total number of padas (micro-divisions) in Vedic astrology?**

A) 27
B) 54
C) 108
D) 360

**Correct Answer: C)** 108 — Each of the 27 nakshatras contains 4 padas (27 × 4 = 108).

## Part 2: Houses and Life Areas

### Question 5
**Which house represents career and public status?**

A) 4th house
B) 7th house
C) 10th house
D) 1st house

**Correct Answer: C)** 10th house — The 10th house (Medium Coeli or MC) represents career, public reputation, and achievement.

---

### Question 6
**The 7th house is associated with which life area?**

A) Home and family
B) Partnerships and marriage
C) Creativity and children
D) Health and service

**Correct Answer: B)** Partnerships and marriage — The 7th house represents one-on-one relationships, including marriage and business partnerships.

---

### Question 7
**Which house represents self-identity and physical appearance?**

A) 1st house
B) 5th house
C) 8th house
D) 12th house

**Correct Answer: A)** 1st house — The 1st house (Ascendant) represents your identity, physical body, and how you appear to others.

---

### Question 8
**The 4th house is associated with:**

A) Communication
B) Home and family roots
C) Travel and education
D) Friendships

**Correct Answer: B)** Home and family roots — The 4th house represents home, family, roots, emotional foundation, and the mother.

## Part 4: Aspects and Relationships

### Question 13
**What is a "conjunction" in astrology?**

A) Two planets 90° apart
B) Two planets in the same sign/degree
C) Two planets opposite each other
D) Two planets 120° apart

**Correct Answer: B)** Two planets in the same sign/degree — A conjunction occurs when planets are close together (within ~10°), blending their energies.

---

### Question 14
**A trine aspect represents:**

A) Challenge and tension (90°)
B) Flow and harmony (120°)
C) Intensity and opposition (180°)
D) Opportunity (60°)

**Correct Answer: B)** Flow and harmony (120°) — Trines (120° apart) are harmonious aspects where energies support each other.

---

### Question 15
**What color represents a "square" aspect in AstroLens?**

A) Blue
B) Green
C) Red
D) Purple

**Correct Answer: C)** Red — Squares (90°) are challenging aspects shown in red. Trines are blue, sextiles are green, oppositions are purple.
