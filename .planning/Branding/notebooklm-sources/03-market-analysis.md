## Risk Analysis

### Technical Risks
**AR Performance:** Older iPhones may struggle with complex 3D
- *Mitigation:* Fallback to simpler visualizations on older devices

**AI Tutor Hallucination:** Risk of incorrect astrological information
- *Mitigation:* Tool-calling only (no text generation), grounded in chart data

### Market Risks
**Competition:** Larger players could copy AR features
- *Mitigation:* First-mover advantage, proprietary visual grammar, network effects

**Astrology Skepticism:** Mainstream perception of astrology as pseudoscience
- *Mitigation:* Position as "symbolic system" not "fortune-telling", focus on self-discovery

### Business Risks
**User Acquisition Cost:** AR apps typically have higher CPI
- *Mitigation:* Viral sharing features, influencer partnerships, App Store featuring

**Retention:** Astrology apps often have low long-term engagement
- *Mitigation:* Time scrubber for ongoing relevance, transit tracking, relationship features
