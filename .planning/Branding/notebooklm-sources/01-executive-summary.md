# AstroLens Executive Summary

## Company Overview

**AstroLens** is an iPhone-first AR astrology application that transforms birth charts into living, navigable 3D visualizations. Founded in 2024, the company is currently in pre-launch phase targeting a Q3 2024 App Store debut.

**Tagline:** See Astrology in AR

**Industry:** Spiritual Technology / Wellness Tech
**Platform:** iOS 17+ (SwiftUI + RealityKit + ARKit)
**Business Model:** Freemium (Free download with premium subscription)

---

## The Opportunity

The astrology app market is valued at $2.2B (2024) and growing at 15% CAGR. However, existing solutions fall into two categories:

1. **Social astrology apps** (Co-Star) - shallow, text-heavy, no visualization
2. **Professional tools** (TimePassages) - accurate but overwhelming for beginners

**The Gap:** No app teaches visual intuition. Users want to *see* and *understand* their charts without studying astrology.

---

## Our Solution

AstroLens introduces the **Grand Clock** metaphor—a 5-ring 3D visualization that makes birth charts tangible:

- **Ring 1:** Self (Ascendant) at center
- **Ring 2:** Houses - 12 translucent wedges
- **Ring 3:** Signs - 12 zodiac arcs
- **Ring 4:** Nakshatras - 27 lunar divisions
- **Ring 5:** Padas - 108 micro-divisions

Users explore their chart through AR at multiple scales: Palm, Desk, Room, or Dome.

---

## Key Innovation: AI Visual Tutor

Unlike competitors that provide text interpretations, AstroLens uses tool-calling AI to:

- Control the 3D scene (highlight, zoom, animate)
- Guide users through visual exploration
- Teach astrology concepts by showing, not telling
- Never hallucinate chart facts (grounded in structured data)

- **SwiftUI:** Native iOS interface
