# AstroLens Brand Voice & Messaging Guide

## Brand Essence

**Archetype:** Sage-Explorer  
**Sage:** Wisdom, knowledge, teaching  
**Explorer:** Discovery, innovation, pushing boundaries

**Personality:** Grounded yet wondrous, technical yet accessible  
**Energy:** Calm, contemplative, awe-inspiring  
**Formality:** Modern professional with warmth

---

## Voice Attributes

### 1. Grounded
Speak with authority but not mysticism. Explain the "what" and "why" without invoking cosmic forces.

**✅ Do:** "Saturn is in your 10th house of career, suggesting themes of discipline in public roles."

**❌ Don't:** "Saturn's karmic energy is challenging your life path in mysterious ways."

### 2. Wondrous
Convey awe at the beauty of the system without being fluffy.

**✅ Do:** "Look at how Jupiter's position creates a flowing trine to your Sun—this is where expansion meets identity."

**❌ Don't:** "The universe has blessed you with Jupiter's abundant cosmic rays!"

### 3. Educational
Teach, don't tell. Every explanation builds understanding.

**✅ Do:** "The 7th house represents partnerships. See how it's positioned opposite your Ascendant?"

**❌ Don't:** "Your 7th house means you'll find love soon."

### 4. Technical-but-Accessible
Use precise terms but explain them immediately.

**✅ Do:** "This is your Midheaven (MC)—the highest point in your chart, representing your public career peak."

**❌ Don't:** "Your MC indicates professional success." [user wonders: what's MC?]

## Tone by Context

### Onboarding
**Tone:** Welcoming and reassuring

> "Birth charts can look intimidating. Let's turn yours into something beautiful and understandable."

**Key messages:**
- Acknowledge astrology can feel overwhelming
- Promise visual simplicity
- Guide step-by-step

### Feature Explanation
**Tone:** Demonstrative and clear

> "Tap any planet to see its house, sign, and relationships. The beams show you the connections."

**Key messages:**
- Show, don't just tell
- Connect to user benefit
- Use spatial language

### AI Tutor Interaction
**Tone:** Patient mentor

> "See that golden orb? That's your Sun in Leo. Notice how it's connected to that beam going to Jupiter? That's a trine—an easy flow of energy."

**Key messages:**
- Answer plainly
- Reference the visualization
- Encourage exploration

### Error States
**Tone:** Helpful and calm

> "We couldn't access your camera for AR. You can still explore your chart in desk mode—tap here."

**Key messages:**
- No blame
- Clear next steps
- Maintain wonder

### Social Sharing
**Tone:** Inviting and intriguing

> "This is what my cosmic blueprint looks like in AR. Finally understand what all those symbols mean."

**Key messages:**
- Spark curiosity
- Don't explain everything
- Use visual language

## Word Choice

### Words We Use

**Visual Language:**
see, look, visualize, observe, chart, map, blueprint, reveal, illuminate

**Spatial Language:**
center, position, placement, orbit, layer, ring, floating, spatial, dimensional

**Educational Language:**
represents, indicates, suggests, corresponds to, symbolizes, reveals, shows

**Experience Language:**
explore, discover, journey, navigate, experience, immerse

### Words We Avoid

**Mystical Mumbo-Jumbo:**
cosmic energy, vibrations, alignment, divine, spirit guides, fate, destiny, aura

**Fortune-Telling Language:**
prediction, will happen, destined, fated, foretold, prophesy

**Vague Woo:**
manifestation, universe is telling you, signs from above, meant to be

**Judgmental Terms:**
good, bad, difficult placement, afflicted, debilitated, cursed, lucky

**Overly Technical:**
ecliptic, celestial sphere, ephemeris, mundane astrology, obliquity

### Preferred Alternatives

| Instead of... | Use this |
|---------------|----------|
| bad aspect | challenging dynamic |
| good planet | supportive influence |
| afflicted | complex configuration |
| fated | indicated |
| will happen | suggests themes of |
| lucky | favorable positioning |
| bad luck | growth opportunity |
| cursed | intense focus area |

**Messaging:**
- "See it to believe it"

Teach astrology as symbolic language, not fortune-telling.

**Messaging:**

- "Symbolic meaning, not mysticism"
- "Grounded wisdom for modern minds"

## Grammar and Style

### Sentence Structure
- **Length:** Medium to short sentences
- **Complexity:** Should come from ideas, not sentence structure
- **Voice:** Active voice 90% of the time
- **Punctuation:** Use em-dashes for explanations (—) and ellipses for contemplative pauses...

### Formatting
- **Paragraphs:** 2-3 sentences max
- **White space:** Generous—this is a visual app
- **Capitalization:** Sentence case for UI; Title case for feature names
- **Lists:** Bullets over paragraphs when possible

### Examples by Channel

**App Store:**
Headline: See Astrology in AR

## The Voice Test

Before publishing any content, ask:

1. ✅ Would a skeptic feel respected, not mocked?
2. ✅ Would an astrology beginner understand this?
3. ✅ Are we teaching, not telling fortunes?
4. ✅ Are we referencing the visualization, not just describing?
5. ✅ Is the tone calm and contemplative, not hyped?

If you can answer yes to all five, the voice is on brand.
