## 🕐 The Grand Clock — Visual Architecture

### What the user sees

Not a chart. Not a wheel. A living, layered clock with depth — rings you can peel apart, zoom into, and step inside. Every ring is a dimension of meaning. Every ring turns with time.

### The 5 Concentric Rings

| Ring | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| Ring 1 | **SELF** | Ascendant / Lagna anchor at center. A glowing axis-cross. This is "you." |
| Ring 2 | **HOUSES** | 12 thick sectors. Life arenas. Career, relationships, wealth, dharma. |
| Ring 3 | **SIGNS** | Zodiac overlay. The colour and style of each house. Aries = fire-red, Pisces = deep blue. |
| Ring 4 | **NAKSHATRAS** | 27 finer divisions. Where "Vedic" begins to feel different. Star-quality energy. |
| Ring 5 | **PADAS** | 108 micro-segments. Subtle precision. Rarely shown — revealed only on zoom. |

### Planets = Luminous Nodes

Each planet is a glowing orb floating above its ring position at different heights — Sun highest, shadow planets (Rahu/Ketu) as a spine cutting through all rings. Size = strength (Shadbala). Colour = classical planetary colour.

## 🎯 The AI Guided Walkthrough — Scene by Scene

### Step 1 — The Blank Stage

The user points their phone at a surface. A subtle grid materialises. Nothing yet. The AI voice says:

> "Place your hand flat. Your chart is about to take shape above it."

*📽 Scene: Dark void. Faint constellation particles swirling. No chart yet.*

### Step 2 — The Ascendant Ignites

A single golden cross-axis pulses into existence at centre. Bright. Everything else is dark.

> **AI:** "This is your Ascendant — your Lagna. The moment of your birth, fixed in the sky. Everything in your chart is measured from this point. This is where YOU begin."

*📽 Scene: Only the ascendant glows. A gold axis-line. Slow pulse. Nothing else visible.*

### Step 3 — Houses Emerge

One by one, 12 house sectors expand outward from the ascendant cross. Each sector is a translucent wedge with a soft colour gradient. They arrive sequentially — 1st, then 2nd, counting around.

> **AI:** "These 12 sectors are your Houses. Each is a domain of life. The 1st is your body and identity. The 10th — opposite you — is career and legacy."

*📽 Scene: Houses animate in like clock hands appearing. Numbered 1–12 at outer edge.*

### Step 4 — Signs Overlay

A second ring of colour overlays the houses — the zodiac signs. Each house gets a sign colour wash.

> **AI:** "Now watch the houses gain colour. That colour is the zodiac Sign — it tells you HOW energy moves in that part of your life."

*📽 Scene: Sign colours diffuse inward from outer ring. Visual contrast with house layer.*

### Step 5 — Planets Drop In

Planets appear one at a time as glowing nodes, dropping into their houses from above. Each arrival is announced.

> **AI:** "Now the planets arrive. Each is an actor entering a stage. Watch where they land — that is their house." [pause] "Your Sun is here in the 5th — the house of creativity and children."

*📽 Scene: Each planet drops with a subtle impact glow. Position inside house sector is clear.*

### Step 6 — Conjunctions Light Up

Any planets sharing a house fuse into a glow-cluster. A visual merge effect — orbits overlapping.

> **AI:** "When planets share a house, they merge influence. See how these two are fused? That is a conjunction. Their energies cannot be separated in this life area."

*📽 Scene: Conjunction shown as overlapping halos, pulsing together. Not separate nodes.*

### Step 7 — Axes Revealed

The two main axes illuminate as glowing rods cutting through the clock: the 1–7 axis (self vs relationships) and the 4–10 axis (home vs career).

> **AI:** "These are your life axes. The great polarities. What you are vs what the world sees. Where you rest vs what you build."

*📽 Scene: Two luminous rods crossing through the centre. Subtle slow rotation.*

### Step 8 — Aspects / Drishti Beams

Beam lines shoot between planets and houses based on Vedic aspects. Saturn beams appear heavier, Jupiter beams glow warm.

> **AI:** "Planets send influence across the chart — these are aspects. Saturn is casting a disciplining beam onto the 4th house. That affects home and inner peace."

*📽 Scene: Beams are colour-coded by planet. Not all on at once — only the one being explained.*

### Step 9 — Dasha Hand

A separate chronograph-style hand appears, rotating to show the current dasha period. A second inner ring lights up like a second timezone.

> **AI:** "This is your current time activation — the Dasha. Think of it as which planet is conducting your life right now. At this moment, [X] Dasha is running."

*📽 Scene: Dasha ring inside Ring 1. Current planet highlighted with a stronger glow.*

### Step 10 — Transit Weather

An outer halo of moving lights appears — current sky positions. Semi-transparent, distinct from natal.

> **AI:** "Finally — what is happening in the sky right now. These outer lights are current transits pressing on your natal chart. This is where present-tense events come from."

*📽 Scene: Transit ring is cooler-toned, slightly translucent. Arrows show direction of motion.*

### Step 11 — Full Clock, One Visual Centre

Everything is now visible, but the AI highlights ONE strongest story — the most activated part of the chart.

> **AI:** "Your chart is alive. Let me show you the strongest theme right now." [One planet or axis illuminates brighter than all else.] "Ask me anything. Or say — walk me through it."

*📽 Scene: All rings visible. ONE focal point brighter than everything. Rest at 40% opacity.*

## ✋ Interaction Grammar — How the User Touches the Clock

### Tap a Planet

- → Scene dims everything else
- → That planet expands + pulses
- → Its house sector illuminates
- → Its aspect beams shoot outward
- → Its sign layer intensifies
- → Its nakshatra slice highlights
- → Dasha ring shows if it's currently active
- → Side panel slides up: Actor → Stage → Style → Pattern → Influence → Now
- → AI speaks: literal → intuitive → causal

### Tap a House

- → House wedge expands toward user
- → All planets in it glow
- → Planets aspecting it show beams
- → House lord is highlighted elsewhere on clock
- → Sign of the house illuminates
- → AI: "This is the 7th house — partnerships, contracts, known others..."

### Tap an Axis

- → The axis rod brightens, rotates to face user
- → Both ends light simultaneously
- → Planets on both ends cluster together visually
- → AI explains the polarity tension
- → Example: 1–7 axis = "Who you are vs who you attract"

### Pinch to Zoom Ring

- → Pinch inward: collapse to desk-mode summary
- → Pinch outward: expand nakshatra ring visible
- → Double-pinch outward: pada ring emerges (108 micro-segments)
- → Rings separate with parallax depth as you zoom

### Two-Finger Rotate

- → Rotate the whole clock like a steering wheel
- → Ascendant stays grounded — only perspective changes
- → Useful for looking at the opposite side of the chart

### Long Press = Time Scrub

- → A timeline slider appears
- → Drag forward: transits move in real time
- → Planets move across houses live
- → Dasha hand ticks forward
- → AI narrates: "In 3 months, Saturn will enter your 10th house..."

### Scale Switching

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| ✋ **Palm Mode** | Chart fits in your hand. Quick reference. |
| 🪑 **Desk Mode** | Default. 40cm hovering clock on table. |
| 🚶 **Room Mode** | You stand inside it. Houses become translucent room sectors. |
| 🌌 **Dome Mode** | Entire room is the chart. Axes cross the space. Planets float at head height. |

## 🎨 Visual Language — What Everything Looks Like

### Opacity as Importance

- ◆ Highlighted element: 100% opacity, glow halo
- ◆ Connected elements: 70% opacity
- ◆ Background elements: 30% opacity (never hidden, just quiet)
- ◆ Inactive transits: 20% opacity

### Glow as Activation

- ◆ Natal placement: steady warm glow
- ◆ Currently dasha-active planet: pulsing glow (heartbeat rhythm)
- ◆ Transit pressing natal: cooler flickering glow
- ◆ Conjunction: merged breathing halo (two orbs sharing one light)
- ◆ Retrograde planet: glow rotates backward / counter-spin particle

### Beam Types (Aspects)

- ◆ **Jupiter aspect:** warm amber beam, gentle curve
- ◆ **Saturn aspect:** cold blue-grey beam, rigid straight line
- ◆ **Mars aspect:** red-orange beam, arrow-tip pulse
- ◆ **Rahu/Ketu:** no beam — they create an eclipse shadow across what they touch

### Axis Visual

- ◆ **1-7 axis:** gold rod (Self ↔ Other)
- ◆ **4-10 axis:** silver rod (Roots ↔ World)
- ◆ **Nodal axis (Rahu-Ketu):** dark spine with smoke trails
- ◆ When activated: axis rod slowly rotates, particles trail off ends

### Ring Separation (Depth)

- ◆ **Houses:** lowest ring, anchored to surface
- ◆ **Signs:** 2cm above houses (in AR space)
- ◆ **Nakshatras:** 4cm above
- ◆ **Planets:** float at 6–10cm (height = strength)
- ◆ **Transits:** outermost ring at 12cm, slowly orbiting

## 🗣 The AI Tutor — Voice + Visual Co-Pilot

### The Tutor's 3 Modes

#### Literal
> "Saturn is placed in the 10th house in Aquarius, in the Shatabhisha nakshatra, pada 2."

*Tone: Factual. Dictionary-style. Good for reference.*

#### Intuitive
> "Discipline, structure, and responsibility shape your public role. You earn authority slowly but durably. People see you as serious."

*Tone: Felt meaning. Lived experience language.*

#### Causal
> "This matters especially now because your Saturn mahadasha began 2 years ago, and transiting Jupiter is aspecting your natal Saturn — this is an unusually activated window."

*Tone: Why now. Why this. What changed.*

### Voice Commands That Reshape the Scene

| Command | Result |
|---------|--------|
| "Show me only career things" | 10th house + lord + aspecting planets light up, rest dims |
| "Why is this conjunction important?" | Scene isolates the cluster, beams show, AI explains fusion |
| "Explain like I know nothing" | Walkthrough restarts from Step 1 |
| "Where is the tension?" | Most stressed axis or square highlights |
| "Is this natal or transit?" | Scene separates layers — natal glows warm, transit glows cool |
| "Zoom into the pada" | Pinch simulation, 108 micro-ring expands |
| "Compare D1 and D9" | Split-ring overlay, D9 appears as ghost ring at same scale |
| "Walk me through my biggest theme" | AI chooses one story, guides start to finish |

### The Saturn Tap — Full Example

User taps Saturn.

**Scene:** Everything dims. Saturn expands with dark-blue glow. Two beams shoot toward the 1st and 7th. The 10th house sector warms up. The Rahu-Ketu axis subtly highlights.

**AI (Literal):** "Saturn in the 10th, Aquarius, Shatabhisha nakshatra."

**AI (Intuitive):** "Saturn here is the taskmaster of your public life. Career comes through discipline, not luck. Recognition comes late but sticks."

**AI (Causal):** "Right now this planet is doubly activated. You're in Saturn mahadasha. And transiting Jupiter is on it. This is a pivotal 18-month window for your career — what you build now will define the next 7 years."

## 🔭 Divisional Chart Lenses — Not Separate Screens

### The Core Insight

Divisional charts are NOT separate apps. They are **lenses** on the same clock. The user should never feel like they "left" the chart.

### Available Lenses

| Icon | Lens | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| ⬤ | **D1 — Rashi** | The base clock. Everything visible. |
| 💍 | **D9 — Navamsa** | Relationship + dharma lens. Houses subtly remap. New house emphasis glows. |
| 🏛 | **D10 — Dashamsa** | Career lens. 10th house architecture expands. |
| 🌱 | **D7 — Saptamsa** | Children lens. |
| 🏠 | **D4 — Chaturthamsa** | Property/assets lens. |
| ♾ | **D60 — Shashtiamsa** | Karma/past life depth layer. Advanced. |

### Lens Transition Effect

When switching from D1 to D9: the outer rings ripple like water. Planets smoothly animate to their new positions. The clock STAYS — only the interpretation layer changes.

> **AI announces:** "Now viewing your relationship and dharma chart. Watch where the planets move."
